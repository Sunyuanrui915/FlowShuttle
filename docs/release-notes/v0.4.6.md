# Flow Shuttle v0.4.6 Release Notes

流梭 Flow Shuttle v0.4.6 修复工作项历史记录误提示，并加强 AI 请求、图片附件和反馈服务的安全性与稳定性。

## 修复与改进

- 新建工作项只填写并保存今日变更摘要后，不再将当天摘要误提示为可恢复的历史内容；更早的每日记录和完整稿快照仍可恢复。
- AI 服务返回异常大的内容、持续无响应或长时间无法结束时，应用会停止请求并保留原文；正常长文本润色继续支持，结果不会被静默截断。
- 保存图片附件时检查附件目录，防止已有符号链接或 Windows 目录联接把图片写到附件目录之外。
- 为反馈服务增加上传存储、提交总量和邮件通知限额，并清理过期的限流记录，降低异常提交对服务器和正常反馈的影响。

---

## English

Flow Shuttle v0.4.6 fixes a misleading work-item history prompt and improves security and reliability for AI requests, image attachments, and the feedback service.

### Fixed and improved

- Saving only today's change summary on a new work item no longer presents it as recoverable history. Earlier daily records and full-content snapshots remain recoverable.
- AI requests stop safely on oversized responses, prolonged inactivity, or excessive duration while preserving the original text. Normal long-text polishing remains supported, and results are never silently truncated.
- Image attachment creation checks the managed directories to prevent pre-existing symbolic links or Windows junctions from redirecting writes outside the attachment root.
- The feedback service bounds retained storage, accepted submissions, and email notifications, and expires inactive rate-limit records to reduce resource abuse.
